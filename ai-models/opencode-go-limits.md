# OpenCode Go limits (Go, $10 a month)

Copied from the owner's view of the OpenCode Go page on September 30, 2026. The
page shows Go and Go Plus side by side. Only the smaller Go numbers are kept. Go
Plus is about 4 times larger for most models. These are the provider's estimates.

| Model | Est. requests per 5 hours | Monthly usage cap |
|---|---:|---:|
| Kimi K3 | 110 | $15 |
| Qwen 3.8 Max | 160 | $15 |
| GLM 5.3 | 220 | $15 |
| GPT 5.6 Luna | 2,050 | $15 |
| MiMo V2.6 Pro | 3,250 | $15 |
| GPT 6 Luna | 4,230 | $15 |
| Qwen 3.8 Flash | 5,400 | $30 |
| GLM 5.3 Flash | 6,320 | $60 |
| DeepSeek V4.1 Flash | 26,000 | $60 |
| MiMo V2.6 Flash | 30,100 | $60 |
| Muse Spark 1.3 Contributor (limited regions) | 45,300 | $60 |

How to read it: more requests per 5 hours for the same plan means the provider
charges less per request for that model. Kimi K3, Qwen 3.8 Max and GLM 5.3 are
the expensive ones. Muse Spark Contributor gives the most requests of all.
